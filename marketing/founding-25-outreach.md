same offer (docs/PRICING-TIERS.md — pricing sync rule applies): **$6/machine
+ $3/tool tag, Operate features included, 12-month price lock, hardware at
cost, month-to-month, cancel anytime.** Two links: **hammertrack.ai/demo** to
show it, **hammertrack.ai/reserve** to close it — the reserve page holds a

The sender is a working GC in the Upstate, not a software vendor. Every
script leans on that — peer-to-peer, not sales-to-prospect.

## 6. Objection one-liners

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I've got LoJack / a dealer tracker on the big iron" | That covers recovery on two machines. This is your whole operation — every truck, attachment, and $400 tool — on one map, plus the alert BEFORE it's gone, not the recovery after. |
| "Tenna/Samsara quoted us" | Great — take their quote, divide by three, and you've got my list price. Founder deal is even under that, locked for a year. And no $500 setup, no contract. |
| "I don't have time to set it up" | It's a plug into the OBD port on trucks and a zip-tie puck on machines. I'll do your first install with you — 15 minutes. |
| "What if you go out of business?" | Month-to-month, cancel anytime, and the hardware is standard Teltonika — it'd work with other platforms. You're risking one month's coffee budget. |
| "Is my data safe?" | Your data's in the same enterprise database stack (Supabase/Postgres) the big guys use, and I'm a contractor — I'm not selling your locations to anybody. |

## 7. The weekly cadence (until the 25 is full)

- **Monday:** 5 texts to contractors you know (script 1 or 2)
- **Wednesday:** 2 dealer/rental counter conversations (script 3)
- **Any theft post in a group:** script 4 within the hour — that window is
  when the whole group is feeling it
- **Every demo given:** email script 5 the same day while it's hot
- Track every send in a note; follow up once after 3 days, once after 10.
  Two touches, then let it breathe.

*First 5 signups are references. Ask each one, after their first theft
alert or first "where's the trencher" save: "Would you tell that story in
one sentence I can put on the site?" — that's the testimonial engine.*
