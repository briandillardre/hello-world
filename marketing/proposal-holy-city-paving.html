HammerTrack is asset tracking built by a contractor, for contractors: live GPS on your trucks, Bluetooth tags on the tools that walk off, after-hours theft alerts to your phone, and job-cost numbers that flow straight toward QuickBooks.

What Holy City Paving gets

🗺️ Live map + full history

Watch all three trucks in real time; scrub back through any day, week, or month. Replay any trip. Weather, radar, and traffic on the same screen your fleet is on.

🚨 After-hours theft alerts

A truck moves at 2 AM — your phone knows before the thief clears the block. Zone-exit and left-site alerts on every job site you draw.

🔧 Tool tags that ride along

Six Bluetooth tags on your high-value tools. Each truck shows what's on board right now, and history answers "which truck had the plate compactor last Tuesday?"

📊 Job-site hours & cost

Draw a zone around each job. HammerTrack logs every hour each machine and crew truck spends inside — the numbers your invoices and QuickBooks job costing want.

🔋 Vehicle health

Low fuel and weak-battery warnings straight from the truck's computer, before the dead morning start.

🤖 Ask in plain English

"Where's the crew truck?" "How long were we at the Meeting St job this week?" The built-in AI answers from your live data.

Getting live — about one morning

  • 1. Plug in the truck trackers. Each one clicks into the OBD port under the dash. First dots on the map within minutes of the first drive.
  • 2. Zip-tie the tags. Name each tool in the app, drop the tag in the box or bolt it on. Done.
  • 3. Draw your zones. The yard and current jobs — two minutes each on the map. Theft alerts and job-hour tracking start immediately.
  • 4. Add your crew. Invite by email; foremen see the map, only you see the dollars.
