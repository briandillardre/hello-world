# Ask HammerTrack — The Questions That Matter

*The AI assistant (the amber **Ask** button) answers from live fleet data —
it calls real tools, computes from the same math as the screens, and never
invents numbers. This is the demo script and the daily habit list.*

*The Founding-25 demo move: hand the prospect your phone and say
**"ask it anything about my fleet."***

## Working today

| Ask it | What it replaces |
|---|---|
| "How long was the excavator at Riverfront last week?" | Calling the foreman, who guesses. Ends billing disputes with timestamped arrivals/departures. |
| "What equipment sat parked all week?" | The idle-capital spreadsheet nobody makes. A $90k machine idle 7 days is a rental to off-hire or an asset to redeploy. |
| "Who showed up at Maple St yesterday, and when?" | Attendance arguments. The data isn't accusing anyone — it's just true. |
| "Any alerts I haven't dealt with?" | The Sunday-night phone sweep. Ten seconds. |
| "How many miles on the Ram this month?" | Mileage logs, IRS substantiation, maintenance timing. |
| "Where is everything right now?" | Opening the map when you just need the sentence. |

## Lights up with crew clock-ins (field ops)

| Ask it | Why it changes the game |
|---|---|
| "Who's on the clock right now and where?" | The dispatcher's whiteboard, dead accurate, from a truck seat. |
| "Summarize yesterday's daily logs" | One paragraph instead of five calls — runs the 7 AM meeting. |
| "Any safety issues reported this week?" | Timestamped safety record = insurance-premium and lawsuit armor. |
| "Total labor hours on Riverfront this month?" | Job costing that reconciles: project hours that sum to payroll. |
