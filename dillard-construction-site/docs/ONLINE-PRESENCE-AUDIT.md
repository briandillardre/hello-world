# Dillard Construction Group — Online Presence Audit
**Date:** July 11, 2026 · **Prepared for:** Brian Dillard
**Scope:** dillardconstructiongroup.com (Wix), all third-party listings, social, and data-broker records — assessed through two lenses: (1) does it win work today, and (2) what does an acquirer see when they Google the company during diligence.

> Method note: the Wix site blocks automated fetchers, so site content was reconstructed from search-engine indexes and cross-referenced third-party sources. Items marked ⚠️ VERIFY should be checked manually in a browser.

## 1. Executive Summary

**Grade: D+ for a company with this much substance.** The underlying assets are genuinely strong — unlimited Group 5 license, Clemson civil-engineering leadership, a USMC-veteran partner who superintended $55M projects, a 4.7-star rating, a 9-county footprint, and a GPS-run fleet no local competitor can match. Almost none of that is visible online. The website is a 6-page Wix brochure with title-tag inconsistencies, uncaptioned photo galleries, typos in the partner bios, and a hedged tagline ("strives to be a top…"). The company ranks for **zero** non-branded search terms. Three major data brokers list a stale address. There is no Google Business Profile visibility, no LinkedIn company page, no press, and no third-party validation layer at all.

For a sale: an acquirer's first diligence act is a Google search. Today that search surfaces a competitor with a similar name (Dillard-Jones Builders), a stale address, an unexplained second brand (dillardpaving.com), and a site that reads "owner-operator," not "professionalized platform." That perception gap is worth real multiple turns at exit.

## 2. Current Website (dillardconstructiongroup.com — Wix)

### Structure & indexed title tags
| Page | Indexed title |
|---|---|
| / | Asphalt Paving and Sitework Contractors \| DILLARD CONSTRUCTION GROUP |
| /about | About \| DILLARD CONSTRUCTION GROUP |
| /services | SERVICES \| Dillard Construction |
| /contact | CONTACT \| Dillard Construction |
| /request-an-estimate | CONTACT \| Dillard Construction *(duplicate of /contact)* |
| /projects | Projects - DILLARD CONSTRUCTION GROUP |
| /photos | Photos - DILLARD CONSTRUCTION GROUP |

**Findings:**
1. **Three different title-suffix conventions** coexist (`| DILLARD CONSTRUCTION GROUP`, `| Dillard Construction`, `- DILLARD CONSTRUCTION GROUP`) plus mixed casing — hand-edited Wix sloppiness that reads unprofessional in search results.
2. **Duplicate titles** on /contact and /request-an-estimate — a duplicate-content signal.
3. **Projects and Photos are redundant nav items** and appear to be bare image galleries with **no crawlable text** — no project names, scopes, or locations. Zero SEO value, zero proof value.
4. **Copy errors in live text:** "formally" (should be "formerly") in Nate's bio; "typical scope … include" (subject-verb disagreement).
5. **Hedged positioning:** the homepage meta description says DCG "**strives to be** a top asphalt paving and sitework contractor" — self-deprecating copy in the single most-read sentence about the company.
6. **Strongest trust signals are buried:** "unlimited Group 5 sitework and paving contractor" sits in services body copy instead of being badged on the homepage. Veteran-owned appears but isn't tied to Nate's USMC story. The 4.7★ rating appears nowhere on the site.
7. **Lead capture is thin:** one generic Wix form + the partners' personal cell numbers. No project-type fields, no bid-invitation path for GCs, no tracked number.
8. **No careers page, no safety page, no team depth, no case studies, no blog** (one indexed SEO post), no visible schema markup.
9. **Interior pages have no keyword or geo targeting** — one site page must carry nine counties of search intent. It doesn't.
10. **Wix bot-wall** returns 403 to many crawlers (even the Wayback Machine has trouble) — link previews and some indexing tools simply can't see the site.

### What the current site does right
- Homepage title is genuinely well-targeted ("Asphalt Paving and Sitework Contractors").
- The partner bios contain excellent raw material (Clemson CE + CBRE; USMC + $2M–$55M superintendent history).
- Service scope copy is specific and complete.
- A contact form exists and both partners are reachable.

## 7. Entity Sprawl (pre-sale structural issue)

A buyer sorting out "what am I buying" will find **three entities sharing Brian's phone number and email domain**:
1. **Dillard Construction Group LLC** — the company (this project).
2. **Dillard Asphalt Paving LLC** (dillardpaving.com) — same principals, same phones, near-duplicate copy ("We strive to be a top Asphalt Paving Contractor in South Carolina"). Splits SEO authority, confuses customers, and muddies asset ownership (which entity owns the contracts? the iron? the DOT number? the reviews?).
3. **Dillard Property Group** — Brian's CRE business (LinkedIn page, LoopNet/Crexi profiles listing the same 864-915-2351).

**Recommendation:** decide dillardpaving.com's fate (301-redirect it into dillardconstructiongroup.com/asphalt-paving is the SEO-correct move unless there's a licensing/insurance reason for the separate LLC), and give DCG its own office phone number so data brokers stop cross-linking the entities.

Also noted ⚠️ VERIFY: a "Nathan Dillard — Caldwell Constructors, Inc." LinkedIn profile appears adjacent to the company in searches. If that's the same Nate, key-person continuity is a diligence question; if not, ignore.
