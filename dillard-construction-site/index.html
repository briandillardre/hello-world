Why Dillard

Run like a company twice its size.

Plenty of crews can move dirt. What sets us apart is how the business behind the iron is run — engineering-led estimating, superintendent-grade field management, and a technology stack most regional contractors don't have.

  • ✓Engineering-led. Founded and run by a Clemson-trained civil engineer — takeoffs, grades, and drainage designs are checked by someone who can read the calcs, not just the plans.
  • ✓Big-project discipline. Our field leadership ran commercial projects from $2M to $55M before founding DCG. That schedule and QC culture comes to every site we touch.
  • ✓GPS-tracked fleet. Every truck, machine, and tool crate is live-tracked with telematics — real utilization data, preventive maintenance on schedule, and equipment that shows up when the schedule says it will.
  • ✓Self-performed scope. Our crews, our equipment, our schedule. No waiting on a paving sub to fit you in.
