Our Story

Founded 2018. Built like it plans to outlast us.

Dillard Construction Group started in 2018 with a simple observation from both sides of the industry: developers were tired of stitching three subcontractors together to get one site built, and good field crews were tired of working for companies that ran on guesswork.

So we built the contractor we wished existed — one company that self-performs the full package from clearing to striping, prices work off engineering instead of instinct, and runs its fleet, maintenance, and job costing on modern software instead of a whiteboard.

Today DCG serves nine Upstate counties from our Greenville headquarters, carries a 4.7-star customer rating, and holds an unlimited license because the work kept getting bigger. The plan is simple: keep doing what got us here, at whatever scale the Upstate keeps growing.
