Technology-Forward Operations

The only Upstate contractor running on its own software.

DCG's entire fleet — trucks, machines, and tool crates — runs on a live GPS and telematics platform built in-house. It's not a gadget; it's how the company operates:

  • ✓Every asset on a live map. Trucks via OBD telematics, equipment via GPS, small tools via Bluetooth tags. Dispatch knows, not guesses.
  • ✓After-hours theft alerts. If a machine moves at 2 AM, our phones know before the thief clears the gate. Equipment theft is an industry epidemic — our insurance carrier loves us.
  • ✓Preventive maintenance on autopilot. Service schedules keyed to real engine hours and mileage, with full service history per machine.
  • ✓Utilization and job costing. Engine hours, idle time, and miles per job site feed straight into QuickBooks job costs — so estimates are calibrated by data, not memory.

For clients, that means machines that show up running and schedules that hold. For our crews, it means good iron. And it means DCG's operations are documented, auditable, and transferable — the company runs on systems, not tribal knowledge.
